Privacy Policy — Run, Demon King!（快跑吧魔王）

Effective date: November 6, 2026 · Developer: Hu Fangjie (胡方捷） · Contact: 360524430@qq.com

English

We do not collect any personal information. Run, Demon King! is a fully offline, single-player game:

  • No account or sign-in is required, and none is offered.
  • The game makes no network requests and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • Your game progress, settings, and collection data are stored only on your device (in the app's local storage) and are never transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • You can erase all locally stored data at any time from Settings → Clear Save Data inside the game, or by deleting the app.
  • The game contains no in-app purchases. The in-game gacha uses only virtual currency earned through gameplay.

If this policy changes in a future version, the updated policy will be posted on this page before that version is released. Questions: 360524430@qq.com.
