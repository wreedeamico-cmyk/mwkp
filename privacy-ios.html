Privacy Notice

No personal data

This game has no accounts, logins or sign-ups. It does not collect any personal information such as your name, email, device identifiers or location.

No analytics, no ads

This game has no ads or third-party analytics. Gameplay works offline; fetching products, payments and unfinished purchases require a connection to the Apple App Store.

In-app purchases

Diamonds are one-time consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. We do not receive bank card details. Transaction IDs, product IDs, diamond balances and spend records are stored on this device to prevent duplicate delivery; they are not uploaded to a developer server.

Purchased diamonds and saved progress

Diamonds do not expire. Resetting progress keeps unspent purchased diamonds. Deleting or reinstalling the app cannot automatically restore completed consumable purchases or balances. Device backups may include saved data but purchase recovery is not guaranteed.

Where your progress is kept

Game progress and settings are stored only in this app on your device and are not uploaded. Your device or iCloud backups may include these files, depending on your system settings. Deleting the app removes local data; Clear Save resets gameplay progress while keeping unspent purchased diamonds.

Distributed via the App Store

This game is distributed through the Apple App Store. If you choose to share analytics with developers in your iPhone's Privacy & Security settings, Apple may give the developer anonymous crash and usage statistics; that is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and the game itself collects no such data.

Contact

Questions? Please contact us through the developer website or support link on the game's App Store page.
